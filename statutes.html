The eleven instruments: statutes, regulations and guidance

Each page explains one law or guideline in plain language, says what it requires and what it leaves out, and shows where it aligns with or diverges from the other side. Every page ends with its source.

A reading that is my own inference, which no cited source states, is labeled “my reading.”

United States
InstrumentWhat it isStatus or date
FD&C Act §524B
Binding law. Effective Mar 2023.
If a medical device runs software, can connect to the internet and has features that could be vulnerable to attack, its maker has to include cybersecurity information when it asks FDA to clear or approve the device.Effective March 29, 2023
FDA guidance on DCT technology
Guidance. Not binding.
Two FDA guidances tell sponsors how to handle technology in decentralized trials.September and October 2024
21 CFR Part 11
Binding rule. In force.
Part 11 sets the conditions under which FDA treats electronic records and electronic signatures as trustworthy and equivalent to paper.In force since 1997 (current text, Oct 13, 2026)
HIPAA Security Rule
Binding rule. In force.
The Security Rule requires safeguards for electronic protected health information held by covered entities and their business associates.Covered entities and business associates
FTC Health Breach Notification Rule
Binding rule. Amended 2024.
Health apps and similar technologies that HIPAA does not cover must notify users, the FTC and sometimes the media within 60 days of a breach of identifiable health information.Amended 2024, effective July 29, 2024
European Union
InstrumentWhat it isStatus or date
MDR Annex I
Binding law. In force.
Medical devices sold in the EU must meet general safety and performance requirements.Applies to devices placed on the EU market
Cyber Resilience Act
Binding law. Main duties Dec 2027.
Makers of connected products sold in the EU must build in security, handle vulnerabilities and report actively exploited ones.Reporting duties from September 11, 2026
GDPR (Arts. 32–33, Ch. V)
Binding law. In force.
GDPR protects the personal data of people in the EU.Applies to any personal data
European Health Data Space
Binding law. Mar 2027; reuse 2029.
The EHDS sets EU-wide rules for using health data in care and for reuse in research and policy.Applies from March 26, 2027
