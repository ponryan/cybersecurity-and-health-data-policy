European Health Data Space: using health data in care and research

Binding Regulation (EU) 2025/327Applies from March 26, 2027Research reuse from 2029; clinical trial data from 2031

In plain language

The EHDS sets EU-wide rules for using health data in care and for reuse in research and policy. For research reuse, data are made available through a secure processing environment under a data permit.

What it requires

A secure place for reuse

Access for secondary use goes only through a secure processing environment that must meet listed security measures. They include access limited to authorized people, identifiable access logs kept for at least one year, and measures against unauthorized reading, copying or removal (Art. 73).

Labels for wellness apps

A wellness application whose maker claims interoperability with an EHR system must carry a label showing compliance with the Regulation’s requirements, valid for up to three years (Art. 47).

Rules for third countries

Transfers of personal data follow GDPR Chapter V (Art. 90). Non-personal data are protected against third-country access that conflicts with EU law (Art. 89). Applications from third countries are eligible only on set conditions (Art. 91).

Reading

My reading: the EHDS secures the pool of data and the environment where it is reused. It sets no security rules for the sensor that produces the data, so a weak sensor upstream can still affect data reused after 2029.

Dates. The Regulation applies from March 26, 2027. Chapter IV on secondary use applies from March 26, 2029, but Article 51(1)(m), data from clinical trials and investigations, applies from March 26, 2031 (Art. 105).

What it does not cover

A wellness device that makes no EHR-interoperability claim does not need the Article 47 label. The data it produces can still become reusable: the categories for secondary use include personal health data generated automatically through medical devices, data from wellness applications, and data from clinical trials (Art. 51(1)(h), (i) and (m)).

U.S.–EU comparison

GDPR aligns. Both protect health data and refer transfers to Chapter V.
U.S. instruments diverge. No U.S. counterpart in this set. The EHDS is the only instrument here that sets up a research-reuse environment.

Margin notes

Two different dates

General research reuse starts in 2029. Clinical trial data as a category starts in 2031. Wearable and wellness data categories start in 2029.

An EU-only step

Nothing in the U.S. set matches this stage of the data’s life.
